# Netflix Customer Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Netflix customer data to show
subscriber distribution, account status, device usage, and content preferences.

## Dashboard Preview

![Netflix Dashboard](Screenshot_2026-08-25_164629.png)

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 957 |
| Active Customers | 676 |
| Total Revenue | 7M |
| Average Watch Hours | 44.28 |
| Average Rating | 3.76 |

## Key Insights

- **Subscription plans are evenly split:** Premium (332), Standard (318), and Basic (307).
- **About 71% of customers are active** (676), while 20% have cancelled (191) and 9% are paused (90).
- **Gaming consoles and Smart TVs are the top devices** (211 and 204 customers), ahead of tablets, laptops, and mobile.
- **Romance and Thriller are the most popular genres** (114 each), followed by Sci-Fi (106) and Drama (100).
- **TV shows are slightly ahead of movies** (51.5% vs 48.5%).
- Customers are spread across multiple countries, with the largest share in North America.

## Features

- Interactive filters for Country, Subscription Plan, Account Status, and Content Type
- KPI cards for customers, revenue, watch hours, and ratings
- Geographic map of customers by country
- Breakdowns by device, genre, and account status
